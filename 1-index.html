Turn your own order records into an organised chargeback evidence pack - before the deadline, without the panic.

      Stripe's current formatting rules

        The PDF is an organiser, not your response itself: it lays out your summary, timeline and which records go where. Your actual records (tracking screenshots, receipts, emails) are never inside it - you attach them, one combined file per type, in your own processor dashboard. You review and submit - this tool never sends anything, and a ticked box only means you said you have the record, not that it meets the network's rules.

        Should you fight this one?

        Just your own numbers - no odds and no predictions anywhere. Dispute fees usually apply win or lose (check your Stripe pricing page for yours), and even strong cases lose sometimes. Only you can judge how strong your evidence is.

        What this tool does - and doesn't

        • Assembles, never invents. The pack contains only facts you entered. Missing items are flagged as gaps, not filled with plausible-sounding text.
        • Your records, your call. Attach only genuine records. Never alter, fake or embellish evidence - that's fraud, and it can cost you your processor account.
        • No outcome promises. The cardholder's bank decides disputes. Nobody can guarantee a win, and this tool doesn't try.
        • Not legal advice. For large or repeated disputes, talk to a professional.
        • Private by design. Nothing you enter is uploaded, sent or saved anywhere - it lives only in this open page and is discarded when you close it. (If you used an earlier version that saved data in this browser, that old stored copy is wiped the moment this page loads.)
        • Protect your customer's data. Your pack will contain personal order details. Redact anything irrelevant before you submit, and only include what the dispute needs.
